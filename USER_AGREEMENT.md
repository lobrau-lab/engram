## 4. Fees and Ten-Percent Royalty

Licensee shall pay the license fee stated in the applicable written
authorization. The proposed royalty is **ten percent (10%) of Gross Revenue**
attributable to the licensed commercial use, and is due only when the completed
commercial schedule in Section 9 expressly includes it and both parties
accept/sign that schedule. The parties should define attribution for bundled

“Gross Revenue” means amounts actually received from customers for products or
services whose sale, delivery, operation, or material commercial value is
substantially enabled by the Software, excluding refunds actually paid and
transaction taxes collected for a governmental authority. This is revenue,
not profit: operating expenses and payment-processing fees are not deductions
unless the written authorization expressly provides otherwise.

Unless otherwise stated in writing, Licensee shall provide quarterly revenue
reports and payment within thirty (30) days after each calendar quarter. The
Owner may, on reasonable notice and subject to confidentiality obligations,
inspect records reasonably necessary to verify royalty statements.
